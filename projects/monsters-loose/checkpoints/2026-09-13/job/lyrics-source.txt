Late-’70s surf punk: scrappy beach-club energy, wiry attitude, clipped attacks and a ragged danceable bounce. Follow the supplied cover’s tempo, groove and musical shape. Bass and dry drums only; evoke surf punk through playing and vocals. Alternate female and male leads, handing off at section boundaries. Female: slack-jawed vowels, sharp consonants, sly melodic talk-singing that slips into rhythmic rap. Male: bratty nasal snarl, clipped rhythmic speech and rough melodic hooks.
