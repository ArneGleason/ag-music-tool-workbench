## What remains provisional

New monster designs, interiors and small props communicate the story but are not automatically approved character or set references. The laboratory group's discussion, the showdown's staged-versus-natural interpretation, and a possible conspiracy outside the fence remain narrative choices. Compare recurring creature details before using any still as a downstream design source. Harper's lasso still needs a dedicated prop/carry/action reference. Generated small logos and sign details are subordinate to the approved brand master.

The holds and cuts help assess narrative emphasis. They do not set the final shot count or suggest that a long scene will be covered by only two moving shots. Final coverage can include overlapping takes, inserts and cutaways as planned.

These are visible roughout differences to resolve through dedicated references, not silent changes to the approved designs.
