# Monsters Loose — Blender lyric animatic v01

Open MonstersLoose-lyric-animatic-v01.blend in Blender. The saved layout has
preview and sequence timeline; Space plays the master. Native text strips are
editable; timeline markers identify bars/beats and lyric pages. Keep this folder
together because the project references master.wav and timing-overview.png.

MonstersLoose-lyric-animatic-v01.mp4 is the full-song 1280x720, 24fps preview.
The artwork supplies the dark teal, cream and warning-red palette. Lyrics use
Barlow Semi Condensed SemiBold, with a restrained entry lift, selected emphasis
and independent word-duration underlines. Upcoming words remain visible in a
muted colour. Caption boxes and speech/thought bubbles are deferred.

The bottom overview shows lead-vocal amplitude through the whole song. Four beat
pads and bar/beat readout follow the locked DAW tempo map. Lead/backing meters
show frame-rate RMS between -60 and 0 dBFS. They are reference measurements, not
invented motion. Master second zero is Blender frame 1 and project bar 4 beat 4.

359 word/syllable candidates plus one unresolved vocal placeholder are displayed
across 47 pages. Original acoustic times and review flags survive in strip custom
properties and animatic-data.json. Some sung endpoints and ad-lib syllables still
need listening review. Page grouping does not change the alignment.
